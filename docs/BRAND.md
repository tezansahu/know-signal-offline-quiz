# Know Signal brand guide

## Name and line

**Know Signal**: *Out of range. Never out of answers.*

**Origin.** The idea came from guest-lecture rooms where the college Wi‑Fi dies, phones show "no signal", and the online quiz tool can't load. Know Signal turns the presenter's laptop into the network: everyone joins its hotspot and plays a live quiz with no internet, no sign-ups and no player caps.

**Why the name works**
- It's a pun on the most dreaded phrase in any lecture hall, **"no signal"**, and it reads as **"know signal"**. Say it aloud and it's the problem; read it and it's the promise.
- It carries both halves of the product. *Signal* is the network the laptop creates; *Know* is the quiz, knowledge put to the test.
- It follows the evocative naming approach that the naming agency Igor recommends (think Amazon or Apple): a metaphor that tells a story, not a description of features.

**Tagline.** *Out of range. Never out of answers.* It flips "out of range", what your phone says when the network dies, into a promise: the room can still play.

**Elevator line.** Know Signal is a live, Mentimeter-style quiz that runs entirely on your laptop. The room joins your laptop's hotspot, scans a QR code and plays. No internet needed.

## Logo

Four rising bars, one in each answer colour (red, blue, orange, aqua), next to the wordmark **know**signal in Archivo Expanded. *know* is set in Black weight and *signal* in Regular, so the quiz half carries the weight.

The mark is deliberately two things at once:
1. **A phone signal meter.** It's the *signal*.
2. **A quiz results chart.** It's what the room *knows*.

The motif recurs across the product: the lobby's signal meter fills as people join, the reveal is a bar chart, and the phone's lobby screen shows the bars pulsing like an equaliser while players wait.

## Type

**Archivo** (SIL Open Font License), bundled so it works offline.
- *Display*: Archivo Expanded Black (`font-stretch: 125%; font-weight: 900`). Scoreboard energy for titles, numbers and the countdown.
- *Text*: Archivo Regular / Bold at normal width.
- Numbers use tabular figures so scores don't jitter while counting.

## Voice

Short, punchy and a little cheeky, like a good quizmaster. Signal-and-network wordplay is welcome when it's light.
- Buttons are verbs with a promise: **Open the room**, **Start the show**, **Reveal the winners**.
- The lobby meter speaks signal: *Searching for signal… → Weak signal → Getting stronger → Strong signal → Full signal*.
- Results celebrate or tease: *Nailed it!*, *Big brain!*, *Plot twist*, *So close*.
- Instructions are plain and kind: *Phone says "No internet"? Tap Stay connected.*
- Never blame the player, never use jargon (no "SSE", "session", "token" in the UI).

## Signature interactions

1. **Two-step join.** Scan the Wi‑Fi QR, then the quiz QR, with a big typeable address as a fallback.
2. **Room signal meter.** The logo bars light up as the room fills, until the room hits *Full signal*.
3. **3‑2‑1 slam.** The question lands first and the options arrive after the countdown, so everyone reads before racing.
4. **Lock-in takeover.** Your chosen answer fills the phone screen. It's final, and it feels final.
5. **Reveal.** Wrong answers fade to grey, the right bar glows, and callouts show *% got it right* and *⚡ Fastest*.
6. **Three-beat standings.** Old order → points count up → rows swap places, with ▲/▼ movement.
7. **Suspense finish.** Standings go dark for the last two questions, ending on a spotlit podium with confetti.
